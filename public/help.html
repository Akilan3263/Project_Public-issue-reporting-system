Help

Use this service to report road issues such as potholes, flooding, debris, road damage, faulty traffic lights, and obstructions.

How to report

  1. Select the issue type and severity.
  2. Enter or detect the location.
  3. Describe the issue clearly.
  4. Add your details or submit anonymously.
  5. Upload any supporting evidence.
  6. Review and submit your report.

For urgent threats to life, call 999.
